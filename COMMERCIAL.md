## Whatever licence you have, BRUTAL's terms also apply

Trinity is built on [BRUTAL](https://github.com/ahwootech/BRUTAL) by Ahwoo Limited, whose own licence covers every product made with it, commercial or not. Trinity handles all three requirements by default, so don't switch them off in a game you release:

1. The BRUTAL logo is shown before gameplay (the engine's splash screen).
2. BRUTAL's copyright notice appears in your credits (`Trinity.Credits.Required`).
3. BRUTAL's `NOTICE.txt` ships next to your executable, along with the third-party notices in `licenses/` (copied there by the build).
