## Techniques the renderer implements

Written for Trinity from published descriptions; no code was copied. Credit where it's due:

- Sky: Sébastien Hillaire, "A Scalable and Production Ready Sky and Atmosphere Rendering Technique" (EGSR 2020).
- Environment specular approximation: Brian Karis, "Physically Based Shading on Mobile" (2014).
- Tone mapping: AgX by Troy Sobotka (the working-space matrices); the ACES curve fit by Krzysztof Narkowicz (2016).

## Tools used at build time (not shipped)

- `glslc` from shaderc (Apache-2.0) compiles shaders to SPIR-V.
- Microsoft.CodeAnalysis (MIT, the C# compiler's API) runs `Trinity.Generators` inside the compiler.
- Blender (GPL) runs `samples/Sandbox/tools/make_character.py` and `make_material_test.py`. Blender's licence doesn't apply to what it outputs.
